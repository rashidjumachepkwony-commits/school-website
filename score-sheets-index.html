How to use: Click "Open Score Sheet" for your class. The sheet loads with the correct subjects and max scores for that class.

Student names: If logged in as admin, student names are pre-filled from the school register. Otherwise, teachers fill in names manually.

Print/PDF: Use the "Download as PDF" button or press Ctrl+P to print. Rows are sized for handwriting.
